# Vectorization assessment for security triage

Use the NPU to find original logs worth reading beside a known example. Keep the fitted text score as the benchmark for ranking possible security cases. A nearby log can show an analyst a variation their search missed. The analyst still needs to read what happened. In one retrieval test, three missed authentication failures surfaced alongside a successful login. [Retrieval experiment](qualification/vector-exploration-contract.md#frozen-engineering-comparison-2026-11-17)

## What an ML caller gets

The 14 November assessment measured a single-graph encoder over the payload. Today's analyst CLI uses a different profile, `f0-s0-mask` from task 2.17. It produces one 128-component `float32` vector per supported original. For N supported records the matrix has shape `N × 128`. Two quantized graphs encode bytes and their patches on the NPU. The adapter pools the real patch outputs, and the comparator normalizes the resulting vectors when calculating cosine distance. Preserve the original record ID and profile revision with each row. The profile accepts nonempty original UTF-8 text up to 1,024 bytes. Longer originals and unusable vectors appear as unsupported. [Earlier encoder](2026-11-14-embedding-qualification.md) · [Encoder implementation](../crates/l2d-encoder/src/hierarchy.rs) · [Profile and input contract](qualification/vector-exploration-contract.md#methods-and-evidence)

The profile repeated every vector and patch boundary across three NPU processes on 25,680 test inputs. Its effective dimension was much smaller than its output width. Across 15 masked-byte fits on CSIC, three to four principal components explained 95% of the variance. In the separate fold-zero, seed-zero diagnostic, a probe using all components recovered endpoint identity with 99.6% accuracy. This helps explain why two records can lie close together when their security outcomes differ. [Stability and geometry measurements](2026-11-15-entropy-encoder-stability-gate.md)

## Where to spend the computation

On the 96-query development panel, the policy made 6,240 encoding calls and took 91.8 seconds end to end. Each query required encoding the 64 history records even though the policy used a nearest-neighbour limit of three. [Measured archive and policy costs](2026-11-18-triager-performance-and-terminal-stack.md#what-the-workstation-measured)

Start with a saved search or a refined pattern, then run `l2d discover explore` on its original members. Search a second saved scope when the question is what the first filter missed. Keep unsupported records, exceptions and a sample from outside the filter available for review. This limits how many originals the product must encode and preserves evidence about what the reduction cost. The current exploration path encodes one original per fixed-shape image and avoids building an all-pairs distance matrix. [CLI and resource bounds](qualification/vector-exploration-contract.md)

For an engineering comparison, align the rows of both representations by original log ID. Tasks 3.1 through 3.6 delivered Rust library measures for population shift, neighbour overlap, label separability, information loss, clustering, vector properties, projection and side-by-side comparison. Before shortening vectors, check which neighbours and which label signal survive. The separability measure holds out individual rows. Transfer to unseen endpoints needs a separate split by endpoint. The assessment, shift and evaluation CLI wiring remains open in task 3.9. ## What would change the decision

Freeze the scorer, retrieval profile and review allocations on an earlier window. Compare score alone, analyst-refined patterns and Venn on a protected later window with independently reviewed outcomes. Count positives outside the seed, misses among unsupported and deferred records, and analyst time at equal review capacity. If the vector-assisted workflow consistently finds additional confirmed cases at that budget and meets the agreed missed-case target, give it a larger role in ranking. Until that evidence exists, score alone is the reference and similar cases are leads for investigation. [Qualification criteria](../.kiro/specs/npu-log-decision/tasks.md)
